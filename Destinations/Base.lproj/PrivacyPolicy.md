# Privacy Policy

**My Little Travel Journal**
Last updated: November 23, 2026

My Little Travel Journal is a personal travel diary. It is built to keep what you write on your own device. This policy explains what the app stores, what it uses, and what it never does.

## What the app stores

- **Places, visit dates and notes** that you type in.
- **Photos** that you choose from your photo library. The app keeps its own copy of each photo you add, so the original in your library is never changed.
- **Postcards** that you create, including the address, sender name and message you write on them.
- **Your settings**, such as the accent color and appearance.

All of this is stored only on this device, inside the app. It is not uploaded to any server and is not synced to other devices by the app.

## Location

When you tap the location button on the "Add a place" screen, the app asks for your location **once**, only to fill in the name of the place where you are. The location is turned into a place name using Apple's mapping service and is then discarded. The app does not save your location, does not track it in the background, and never asks for it unless you tap the button.

## Photos

The app uses the system photo picker, so it can only see the photos you pick. It does not have access to the rest of your library.

## App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is never stored as text. Only a salted hash of it is saved in the device's secure Keychain. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. The only way back in is to erase all data in the app.

## Sharing

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to share it. When you share or export a postcard, the image goes only where you send it, for example to Files, AirDrop or Messages.

## What the app never does

- No accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising and no tracking.
- No analytics and no data collection by the developer.
- No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a place, a photo or a postcard at any time inside the app. Deleting a place also deletes its photos. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update, with a new "Last updated" date.
